# Security

A vulnerability is reported privately, never in a public issue: by email to
anna@bureaudessurfaces.fr, subject "relevé security", or through this
repository's private vulnerability reporting (Security, Report a vulnerability).

In scope: the service at releve.bureaudessurfaces.fr, the observer (`observe/`),
the kernel (`kernel/`), and the scripts that install and route the service
(`serve/`). Out of scope: load alone, and what relevé finds on the sites it
observes.

Each report is acknowledged within three working days, and answered with a fix
or a dated plan within thirty. A fix is published with a note in CHANGELOG.md,
crediting the reporter if they wish.

The service keeps nothing of a request. A report needs only the address given
to the form and what came back.
