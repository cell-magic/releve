Verify a record

Checks a RADAR record against its path, its month's root and the published roots, and its selection against its beacon round. Everything runs in this browser, from this one file; nothing is sent anywhere. Two checks need more than a browser holds, the time-stamp tokens' signatures and the round's BLS signature: this page says so, and python -m record verify makes them.

The .json bundle delivered with the record.
roots.json, as the Bureau publishes it.
